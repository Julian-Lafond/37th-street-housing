## Reasonable accommodations and modifications

If you or someone in your household has a disability, you can ask for:

- **A reasonable accommodation:** a change to a rule, policy, practice or service, such as help completing an application, communication in an alternative format, or an exception to a policy so you can use and enjoy your home.
- **A reasonable modification:** a physical change to an apartment or common area, such as grab bars or a ramp.

You can make a request at any time, before or after you apply or move in, by any method that works for you, including by email, phone, mail or in person. You don’t have to use any particular words or form. We may ask for information needed to evaluate the request, as the law allows, and we’ll keep it confidential. We’ll respond promptly and, if we can’t grant a request as made, work with you to find an alternative that meets your needs.
