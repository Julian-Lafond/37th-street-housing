[OWNER NAME / ENTITY] wants everyone to be able to use 37thstreethousing.com, including people with disabilities.

## Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more usable for people with a wide range of disabilities.

## What we’ve done

Accessibility is built into the Site’s code rather than added with an overlay or toolbar. The Site:

- Uses semantic page structure, headings and landmarks, with a “Skip to main content” link
- Works fully with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator
- Meets WCAG AA color-contrast requirements, and never uses color alone to convey meaning
- Provides text descriptions for every meaningful image
- Gives apartment names with fractions (for example, “Apt 1158 ¼”) a clear spoken name for screen readers
- Labels every form field, marks required fields in text, and explains errors in words, with links to each field
- Respects your device’s “reduce motion” setting by turning off non-essential animation
- Reflows to a single column at 320 pixels wide and at 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling
- Is tested with automated tools (axe-core) and by keyboard

## Known limitations

- **Interactive map:** The optional Google map is provided by a third party, and we can’t guarantee its accessibility. The address and an “Open in Google Maps” link are always available as text.
- **Photos:** Photo descriptions describe what each picture shows. Because photos are representative, they may not show the specific apartment you’re interested in.
